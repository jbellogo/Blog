It is a a theoretical [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer "Computer") design created by Noam Nisan and Shimon Schocken and described in their book, _The Elements of Computing Systems: Building a Modern Computer from First Principles._[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hack_computer#cite_note-1) In using the term “modern”, the authors refer to a digital, [binary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_number "Binary number") machine that is patterned according to the [von Neumann architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_architecture "Von Neumann architecture") model.


Elements:
 - ROM
 - CPU
 - RAM

We essentially will have a [[Software Hierarchy]] sitting ontop of a [[Hardware Platform]]
